How repeat data collection works

A controlled process to update records without losing previous survey history.

01
Create a campaign

Administrator selects target areas and survey period.

02
Visit and verify

Survey officer finds the household using its unique Household ID and checks existing details.

03
Submit changes

Officer records changes such as births, deaths, migration or corrected family counts.

04
Review and update

Authorized supervisor verifies submissions; approved data becomes the latest record.
